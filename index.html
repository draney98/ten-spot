    How to play

    Clear two tiles with the same mascot. An outline and its solid twin match, and so do two the same. They must touch, or have only crossed-out cells between them, including across the end of a row and on a diagonal. Clear the board to reach the next level. Every game climbs one ramp: you start on a small 5 × 5 board, and as you go the board grows, new mascots arrive, brick walls appear, and your buttons shrink. A wall can't be cleared and blocks the way. Its arrows show how it goes: left and right, when the rest of its row is cleared; up and down, when every tile in its column is cleared (tiles you add below count); four separate arrows, when either happens; a cross with a solid centre, only when both do. A wall pays a bonus when it goes, shown under its bricks. The bonus grows with every pair you clear while it stands, and doubles if the wall lasts to the level's final pair. Easy runs to level 36, Normal from 37, Hard from 53. Stuck? Add copies everything left onto the bottom, but only once you are down to your last pairs, and each level allows only a few; from level 30, pressing it too soon counts as a wrong pick. Pack (from level 10) slides the tiles together. If, after an Add or a Pack, the tiles left sit together with no gaps and every one has its match right beside it, the game offers to finish the board for you: a clean sweep, for bonus points. You can say no and play on. Pairs score more the farther apart they are, and double when both are outlines or both are shaded. Clear pairs in a row to build a streak, up to double points. A wrong pick breaks your streak and costs points. Hints and undos cost a few points. Finish a level in fewer matches than par for a bonus. Points grow with every level.
