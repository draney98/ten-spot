How to play

Clear two numbers that are equal or add up to 10. They must touch, or have only crossed-out cells between them, including across the end of a row. Stuck? Add numbers copies everything left onto the bottom, but each level allows only a few. Clear the board to reach the next level: bigger boards, fewer adds, more points. Pairs score more the farther apart they are, and double when both are outlines or both are shaded. Clear pairs in a row to build a streak, up to double points. A wrong pick breaks your streak and costs points. Hints and undos cost a few points. Finish a level in fewer matches than par for a bonus.
