How to play

Clear two numbers that are equal or add up to 10. They must touch, or have only crossed-out cells between them, including across the end of a row. Stuck? Add numbers copies everything left onto the bottom, but each level allows only a few. Clear the board to reach the next level: bigger boards, fewer adds, more points. Pairs drawn the same way (two outlines, or two shaded) earn a style bonus. An outline with a shaded tile still clears, but costs half its points. Picking two tiles that don’t match costs half the points they would have scored.
