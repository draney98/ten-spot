ten-spot tile sheet

Put a tile set on one picture for an image AI, then cut the picture it gives back into the 16 files the game uses. Works for the pixel sets (Pixel, Arcade, Arcade HD). Tuner · Game

1. Combine: make a sheet

A square sheet, 4 × 4: the 8 day tiles on paper in the top two rows, the 8 night tiles in the bottom two. Each cell is enlarged so the AI has room to work. Square, because image AIs handle square pictures best.

Any size is fine as long as the AI kept the 4 × 4 layout. PNG, JPEG or WebP. The red boxes show where the cuts fall; if they miss, use Trim.
